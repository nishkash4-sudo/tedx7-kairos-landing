Act 01 — The theme

The Greeks had two words for time. Chronos — the time that passes. And kairos — the moment that changes everything. This year, TEDx MDI Gurgaon is about the second one.

KAIROS
kai·ros — n.

The opportune, decisive moment; the point at which action changes the outcome. Where Metanoia asked us to change our minds, Kairos asks us to act — six speakers on the moments that cannot wait.
